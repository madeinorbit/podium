Native agent toggle — alignment fix

Session header, apps/mobile at 390×844. Segment control shown at 8× zoom, native view active.

Before / after

The tell is the underscore: before, it sits clearly below the baseline of “Chat”; after, the two labels share a baseline band.

Same control, Chat active

What was wrong

  • The monospace face never applied. The label asked for mono(600) as the first entry of its style array, and styles.segmentText — which carries sans(600) — came after it. Later styles win, so the terminal mark rendered in the proportional Geist face instead of Geist Mono.
  • The cell centres the line box, not the ink. Both cells centre their label identically (measured: text box y=14.5, h=14 in a 26px cell for both). But >_ has no ascender and its underscore sits below the baseline, so its ink lands lower in that box than a cap-height word like “Chat”.

A 1.4px gap between the two ink centres — the whole visible defect.

The fix

One style, in apps/mobile/src/screens/SessionScreen.tsx: put mono(600) after segmentText so the terminal face actually applies, and lift the mark by translateY(-1.5) onto Chat's optical centre. A transform, not margin, so the nudge cannot reflow the 28px strip.

Verified by re-exporting the Expo web bundle and re-measuring: both ink centres now land at 21.6–21.7px against a 21.5px cell centre.
