“Open in explorer” opens the explorer, and nothing else

Clicking a task ref in chat opens the ref card; its “Open in explorer” action used to move the shell’s selection to reach the explorer, which also swapped the sidebar selection and the tab area. Both runs below are the same script against the same instance — only the bundle differs.

The card

The chat mentions POD-1271, a task outside the selected mission. The shell is on POD-1262, with its agent’s tab open in the workspace.

beforeThe whole workspace follows

The sidebar selection jumps to POD-1271, the mission panel switches with it, and the tab area — which is keyed by the shell selection — empties to “Nothing open in this pane”. The chat that was being read is gone.

afterOnly the dock moves

The explorer opens on POD-1271. The sidebar still holds POD-1262, the mission panel and the chat are untouched, and the “Typecheck lane” tab is still open.

How it was verified

  • An isolated from-source instance on port 19388 over a snapshot of the live database, driving a real transcript with real ref chips.
  • The same script on both bundles: the old one reports the tab strip changed, the new one reports it unchanged and the dock on the target task.
  • Unit coverage at both ends: the card’s handler never touches the shell selection, and the explorer accepts a target from outside the selected mission — which the focus route cannot reach at all.
