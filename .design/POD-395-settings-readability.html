Settings screen — readability pass

POD-395 · before / after against a from-source instance, Superade dark, 1680×1000

Seventeen tabs had grown ten font sizes, two greys doing the same job, and six prose measures — so nothing on the screen could be read as a rank, because no two ranks were spelled the same way twice. Four sizes and one measure now carry the whole surface.

The rail

Three visibility classes did not read as three: a group’s mono label sat about 5px under the last row of the previous group and 8px above the first row of its own, so it captioned the wrong block — its 18px top margin collapsed through the wrapper div and never rendered. Each class is now a block with a seam above it, the same device the pane uses between sections. The rail also went 220px → 184px: its longest label measures 95px, so it was more than twice as wide as anything in it, and the 36px goes to the pane.

New sessions

The tab opened with two rules 24px apart and a band of dead air between them — a first: guard on an element that is never the first child. Descriptions wrapped at 44ch in 3.8:1 grey while 180px of the row sat empty; the controls stopped 130px short of the pane edge. Now: one rule, one 62ch measure at 6.2:1, and every control on the pane’s right edge.

Notifications

The switch and its status text touched — the control cell had no gap — and the sentence explaining the sound cues was right-aligned prose inside the control column. The permission state is now the row’s own warning line, so both switches sit on the same edge as every other switch on the screen.

Appearance

“Terminal” was a 13px sub-heading under a 12.5px section heading: the subgroup outranked the section containing it. It is now 12px semibold under a 13px section head, with its own rule. Field values dropped from 14px (the largest text on the screen) to 12.5px.

Experimental

The flag list had its own flex layout, its own sizes and no seams. It is the same Row as everywhere else now — same two columns, same hairline, same measure — and “Reset settings” became a real subsection instead of a hand-rolled bordered div.

Hibernation

The “Maximum idle sessions” explanation lived inside the right-hand control cell, left-aligned, under the input. It is a description, so it now sits under its label; the cap warning uses the --warning token instead of a raw text-amber-600.

Secrets

Row descriptions ran in --text-dim at 11.5px (3.8:1). Every explanation on the screen now runs one size, one ink, one measure.
