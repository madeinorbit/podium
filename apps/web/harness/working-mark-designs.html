Working mark designs

Sixteen motion ideas for the working mark, plus the current still mark and the original wave for reference. These prototypes are for judging the look only; nothing here has been measured yet. Each design moves only pre-drawn shapes (opacity and transform), which is the kind of animation the GPU can run without the page's main thread. The grey tag shows how many moving layers one mark needs; fewer layers is cheaper.

A crowd of them

Thirty-two rows at the real 12 px size, the way a busy sidebar looks. "In step" makes every mark share one clock; "Out of step" gives each its own phase.
