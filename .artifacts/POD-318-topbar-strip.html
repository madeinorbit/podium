What changed, in order of what it buys

  1. A hover you can see. Both chips became 26 px chrome cells with the shell’s accent wash, a distinct pinned rung, and a focus ring that isn’t clipped. The hit target went from a sliver of text to a cell.
  2. Every meter says what it measures. MEM, CC, CX, GR — and QUOTA demoted from a chip-sized word to the group label it always was.
  3. A number beside every bar. 30 px of meter is ~3% per pixel; 75% versus 60% is the entire decision and the bar could not carry it.
  4. Colour only where it’s earned. The number takes warn/crit ink at the thresholds; everything else stays Dim. The model-bucket rail stops impersonating an alarm.
  5. The track became a groove. An empty meter now reads empty.
  6. The two header icon buttons picked up the shared data-pressable contract they were missing — they were the one interaction-contract violation in the app.
