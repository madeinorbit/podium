Agents hang on the tree, not in a box.

The roster band's job is to say “these are execution, and they belong to that issue.” Today it says it with a second container nested inside the issue's own card. A tree guide says the same thing with one hairline, and it keeps saying it as depth grows, which a box cannot. Two rail segments carry it: a neutral one for agents, a colour-tinted one for plan structure, each opened by a mono label and closed by its own elbow. The counters come off the rail entirely, because the issue row already states both.

A

Both counters already live on the issue row. Delete them from the rail.

rowStatusLine emits 2 agents · working · 1/3 subtasks · 12:14 today (derive.ts:2327 and :2331). The rail label repeating · 2 is the same fact twice in one region, which is exactly what the merge chip already suppresses on the git stamp. So the labels become pure section markers: AGENTS, SUBTASKS.

Both words are already the app's own vocabulary, taken from that same status line.

B

A rail segment is bounded by its label and its elbow.

Two sibling .tree blocks instead of one, separated by 5px. The elbow that terminates the agents rail is now doing real work: it says "this group ends here", and the next label opens the next one. No box, no divider line, no extra vertical space beyond the one label.

C

Rail hue says kind. Rail opacity says depth.

The plan segment carries the issue tint at 55%, because sub-issues inherit the issue's identity. The agents segment stays neutral at #2b3550, because execution does not own the issue's colour. Read at a glance and off-label, a coloured rail means structure and a grey rail means agents.

Depth still fades independently: a subagent's rail steps down from its parent's, whichever hue it is.

D

Sub-issues render at full issue weight.

A nested sub-issue is a real UnifiedIssueRow: 26px identity square with its ref printed inside, two lines, its own status and timer. An agent row is one line with an 18px kind chip and a mono session ref on the right. Drawn honestly they were never close; the v1 mock shrank them and manufactured the collision.

The one geometry consequence: a taller row needs a lower elbow, so --tree-elbow is 12px on agent nodes and 18px on issue nodes.

04Five more decisions, and why

Section 02 settled how the two groups separate. These are the rest, and each is a thing to argue with.

01

The border and the fill go; the label stays.

The band carried three signals to say one thing: a border, a tone tier, a mono label. The label already says it in words and gives the count. The other two were a card nested inside a card, which DESIGN.md's Carved Rule forbids on its own terms: if a resting surface needs to differ from its neighbour, change its tone or engrave it, never stack it.

What still does the L2 work: the AGENTS · N mono header, the kind chip instead of an ID square, one-line rows against the issue's two, and the mono ref on the right edge. Four differentiators, zero containers.

02

One rail geometry, reused everywhere under the row.

Today the band sits adjacent to the subtask tree at ml-8, with its own nesting rules, while sub-issues hang off .tree-children at left:16px. Two indent systems inside one unfolded row. Both segments now use the same rail at 16px and differ by hue, label and row weight instead (section 02).

Net effect on width: agent rows start at 36px instead of 42px, so every session name gets 6px back and long coordinator names truncate less.

03

The rail ends where the last child is, and rounds into it.

The current guide is an absolutely-positioned span from top:0 to bottom:12px, a guess that overshoots a short last row and undershoots a tall one. The hybrid draws the vertical on each child and truncates the last one to var(--tree-elbow) with a 6px bottom-left radius. The group's end becomes a fact of the markup, at any child count, with no measurement.

04

Roster rows join the issue's colour channel.

Rail hue is settled in section 02: coloured for plan, neutral for execution. Within either, a subagent's rail steps down 25 points from its parent's so depth reads as recession without a second indent cue.

Roster rows currently opt out of the tint (bg-[#1c2a47] hard-coded) because the band's navy fill was underneath them. With the fill gone they opt in: hover and selection use the issue's --child-hover-bg/--child-active-bg, exactly like sub-issue rows. Row and unfolded block finally read as one coloured card.

05

The ✳ goes. The kind chip is the agent's mark.

Every roster row draws a terracotta ✳ and then a 20px kind chip beside it: two glyphs for one job, and the ✳ is Claude's brand clay even on a Codex row, which the Reserved Hues rule prohibits. Keep the chip (it says which agent), drop the asterisk, reclaim 14px.

The chip itself moves to Chip Navy #16223c from #22222c, a grey predating the navy palette, and from 20px to 18px so it never competes with the 26px identity square.

06

No new motion.

The rail does not draw itself in. Expanding uses the existing arrival treatment on the whole block; the guide arrives with it, then holds perfectly still. Stillness is the "needs you" signal, so the working spinner and the counting timer stay the only perpetual motion on the surface.

05What leaves, what arrives

Everything functional is preserved: close-on-hover, Continue on error, rename on double-click, the context menu, snooze, coordinator badge, draft/unread/outcome chips, stale fold, consumed-children fold.

Removed

  • The band container · rounded-[6px] border border-[#1e2a4c] bg-[#0e1626] px-1.5 pb-1
  • The ml-8 offset, and its second indent system
  • The ✳ roster glyph · redundant with the kind chip, wrong hue off Claude
  • The ml-3 border-l pl-0.5 subagent nest · replaced by the rail
  • Hard-coded roster tones #1c2a47 / #16223c on row hover + active
  • The [data-tree-band] stub exclusion · no longer a special case
  • The rail label's counter · rowStatusLine already states both counts on the row

Added

  • .tree / .tnode · ~20 lines of CSS, no JS, no measurement
  • A second labeled segment · SUBTASKS, bounded the same way as AGENTS
  • Four rail vars · plan and agents, each with a depth-2 step
  • --tree-elbow · row half-height, one tunable number
  • Tint-aware roster rows via the existing --child-*-bg vars
  • role="group" + aria-label="Agents"; rails are aria-hidden
  • 18px Chip-Navy kind chip replacing the 20px #22222c one

07Three things to decide before I build

01Does SUBTASKS earn its own label?

The gap, the hue change and the full-weight rows may already bound the agents group on their own, in which case the second label is one more line of chrome on every issue that has both.

Recommendation: keep it. Symmetry is what makes two segments read as two groups; an unlabeled tail reads as leftovers from the first one.

02Does the ✳ go in this change or a separate one?

Dropping it is the single most visible row-level edit here, and it is technically independent of the container question.

Recommendation: same change. Without the band's fill the rows have to carry themselves, and a duplicate glyph is exactly the noise that makes an unboxed row look unfinished.

03Does the unowned-worktree band keep its box?

It is top-level and selectable. There is no parent row for a rail to hang from, and its hairline firms on selection. Removing the box there costs a real affordance.

Recommendation: keep the box there, label becomes the parent node. The rule is “no container nested inside another container”, not “no containers”.
