POD-1129 · review

Phone close guard

Marking a task Done from the phone used to call closeIssue straight through — retiring pending agent decisions and walking away from uncommitted work without saying so. Both phone paths now raise the desktop's guard, over the desktop's derivation, in the phone's own grammar.

The decision you made

Cheap when clean, a stop when there's something to lose

The press closes instantly when the shared derivation finds no blockers. The sheet rises only when there is a cost to name. The guard exists to state what closing would throw away — a sheet that rises to report nothing at stake taxes the most ordinary action on the surface where taps are dearest.

[Done] ──────────────► closed

One tap. Same as before this change.

guarded Work at stake

[Done] ──► sheet ──► Close anyway └─ Keep open ──► nothing

Two taps, and the second one is informed.

Every line comes from the desktop

Labels and detail sentences are the desktop dialog's, word for word, because they are the same function's output. Only the presentation is native — an inset group with hairlines instead of an AlertDialog.

Two layout rules, both about a moving thumb

  • Keep open is bottom-most, where every sheet in the app puts Cancel. The sheet rises from the bottom edge, so a tap that beats the animation lands on the safe row or the backdrop — never on Close anyway.
  • Concerns sit above the destructive row, so the more there is to lose, the further that row travels from wherever the last tap landed. The interruption scales with the cost.

Rendering built from the component's own style values and copy strings — not a device screenshot. The mobile test lane cannot render a real sheet (filed as POD-1143).
