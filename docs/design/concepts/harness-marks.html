Paint, not geometry. It references the shipped symbols in AgentIcons.tsx unchanged and adds only a CSS treatment, so a new harness gets its ghost the moment its icon lands. The brand fills — #D97757 and the rest — are dropped for the surrounding text ink, which is what keeps one opacity reading correctly on paper and in the dark, and what stops five harnesses turning the pane into a colour chart.

Where it does not appear. It is gated on the asking state — the one that has a question to sit behind. That also excludes shell (it never asks, and its glyph is a lucide terminal rather than a brand mark), a session that stopped without writing a transcript, and the orchestrator, which is not a harness — the same reason its coordinates line is suppressed.

In flow, not floated. The mark occupies real space above the question rather than being absolutely positioned into the empty scrollport: the feed is the scroll container, and anything overflowing past its start edge is clipped and unreachable.
