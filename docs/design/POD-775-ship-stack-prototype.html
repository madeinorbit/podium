Interaction principles

What the interface is trying to make true

01 · Custody

The click changes ownership

“Ship” is not another message to a tired agent. It gives Podium a durable order and immediately tells the human who owns the next step.

02 · Proof

Show the thing before the policy

The live preview or best artifact leads the review card. SHA-bound validation and destination policy remain visible without becoming the headline.

03 · Quiet

Progress does not become noise

Routine steps live in the Stack. The Tray contains decisions and destination receipts, not a notification for every fetch, test, and retry.

04 · Pushback

One hold, one real choice

Podium reports what code and models already tried, isolates the smallest interaction set, and offers actions that state their consequence.
