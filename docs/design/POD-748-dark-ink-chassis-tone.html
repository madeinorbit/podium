Why the ground was washed. POD-725 made this element the ground the sheet lies on and gave it a flat var(--background) in styles.css. The old 12%/9% issue wash stayed behind in index.css, and its slate half was written as [data-issue-colored='false'] .native-agents-pane — a 0-2-0 selector that outranks the 0-1-0 replacement no matter which file loads last. So a mission with no colour got the wash and a coloured one got the mock's value. The wash is deleted, not overridden.

Why the strip sat on top. Every issue-mix-* walks its surface toward a lighter colour, and Dark Ink leaves only three levels between the strip and the sheet it is cut into. The focused dose of 6/4 spent five, so the recess rendered as a raised band. It is now 3/2 focused and 1/0 unfocused — the most focus can say here without inverting the ramp. The drag-target state keeps its loud 24/18, which is the one moment it should shout.
