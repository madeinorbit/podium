Quota & load popovers, redesigned

The topbar's two health chips — machine load and agent quota — currently answer hover with a prose tooltip and click with a generic centered modal. This concept replaces both with one shared instrument anatomy at three zoom levels: chip → hover panel → pinned breakdown.

Tier 0The chips (unchanged footprint, corrected tone ramp)

The 44px header keeps its 34×3.5px meters. One fix: the ok state currently renders green (bg-success) — Superade has no green. The ramp becomes blue (ok) → yellow (≥75%) → red (>90%), matching the theme's "blue is the calm all-good."

Board · Sessions · Usage podium macbook quota

Hover behavior: the chip gains the standard chip-navy wash (shown on “quota”), and the panel below opens after 250 ms — it replaces the text tooltip entirely. Click pins the same panel expanded; Esc or outside-click dismisses.

Tier 1Hover — quota

Today hover produces a sentence: “Claude Code (till@…) — 5h 68% · wk 41%”. The redesign keeps every number but gives it the same grid the breakdown uses: mono window label, bar with an elapsed-time tick, tabular percent + reset. The tick against the fill is the pace signal — fill past the tick means burning hot, no extra words needed at this tier.

Anatomy

Header verdict answers the only question that matters at a glance: will quota get you to the reset? Derived from the worst window’s pace — lasts until reset, or 5h window won’t last when any window burns hot.

The white tick is the pace reference: it marks how much of the rolling window has already elapsed. Fill short of the tick = usage slower than time (safe); fill past it = burning faster than time.

Account groups separated by hairlines — agent name in sans (human voice), plan as an engraved mono tag, email truncated right.

Window rows on a fixed 3-column grid so bars and numbers align across accounts.

Footer in micro mono label — replaces the “Click for the breakdown” body-text line.

Hibernation status compresses to one status line.

Choices

Hostname is the title, numbers move to the mono slot — machine voice on the right, human voice on the left, consistently with the quota panel.

Segment hues: agents take yellow (the thing Podium runs — your attention’s subject), projects blue, other a neutral border tone. Same mapping in the pinned tier and the modal today.

Warning states recolor only the chip meter and the % readout; the segments keep their identity hues so composition stays readable under pressure.

Tier 2Click — pinned breakdown, not a modal

Click currently teleports to a centered dialog, severing the spatial link to the chip and dimming the whole board. The redesign pins the hover panel in place and extends it — same anchor, same width family, now interactive (scrollable, links, live-updating). No backdrop: the board stays visible, which is the point of a health surface. The quota tier adds the pace verdict and machines; the load tier adds per-process rows and the hibernation controls. Depth of the transition: ~180 ms height ease-out, reduced-motion → instant.

Pace chips appear only at this tier and only where they change a decision — “won’t last” in yellow (attention being asked → the Signal Rule permits it), “headroom” in calm blue, “on pace” omitted entirely: silence is the default state. Degraded accounts (expired, error) collapse to a single dim line instead of an empty card. Connection stops being a co-equal tab; it’s a footer jump, since it has its own indicator when unhealthy.
