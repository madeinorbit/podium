# POD-4754 — progress loads a drawn row's closed family on demand

Operator decision 2026-09-28: the "done of total" count loads the closed
sub-issues it needs on demand; no server-maintained counters. It replaces the
2026-09-24 ruling "option A", under which progress read a cold child's
`stage` / `closedReason` synchronously by id (`visibleInputs.progressFacts`,
the pool's `row(…, 'peek')`) without loading it.

## What changed

- `worklist/rollup.ts`: a formal child's unit (`unitOwnPartOf`) reads its row
  through the one row reader (`RollupInputs.loadedIssue`, `MobxPool.row` in
  `load` mode). A cold row answers `LOADING`, its load is queued, and the unit
  is a pending marker (`PENDING_UNIT`). `unitsBelowPartOf` does not read a
  cold child's own closure until it lands, so a deep closed chain asks for one
  level per window. `Units.pending` sums the markers; the row's `loading` is
  set while any attention or progress marker is pending.
- `progressFacts` is gone from `RollupInputs`, `VisibleInputs`
  (`worklist/visible.ts`), the pool (`pool.ts`) and the rebuild
  (`rebuild.ts`). No new caller of `peek`.
- The bound is the reads: a drawn row's progress reads its formal children's
  units, so drawing it queues exactly its cold children; a row nobody draws is
  read by nobody and loads nothing.
