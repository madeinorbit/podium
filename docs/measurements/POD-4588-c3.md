## 3. Fixes (STEP 2)

Dispose-path review (the issue's "typical" leaks): `arm.dispose` unsubscribes
both channels, unmounts and clears all roots, then `pool.dispose` disposes
every IssueCells/session cell/ids cell BEFORE clearing tables
(`VisibleCollection.clear`, `RollupCollection.clear`, `WorklistGroups.clear`
each dispose their cells and drop their maps), clears tables, records,
readers, presence, engine, residency (timer cancelled, queue and registry
cleared), coldness/coldRows/moves, membership, selection, clock, graph,
listeners. No try/catch control flow on the bootstrap path (throws are
exceptional-only; `cells.ts` uses try/finally for drain flags; the heap
confirms zero `CaughtException` objects). The per-sample survivor fence
(WeakRefs on runtime, store, replica, cache, arm handle, row source) passed
all 20 switches with zero survivors and the switch heap growth is within
(0.951 ≤ 0.984): no listener, model or registry leak found to fix.

The three misses stay with their mechanisms (Mc3 precedent: recorded, not
chased):

- coldBootstrap wall (2.47x, budget 1.1x) and retained heap (3.43x, budget
  1.1x): construction — §2-top + heap table. The pool builds visibility and
  filing state for every known issue (4,867) before first paint where the
  control does a plain one-pass compute over the visible set (buildMs 386.8
  vs 102.2). Making filings lazy is a redesign in `worklist/rollup.ts` +
  `visible.ts` (POD-4694's open issue): reported, not touched here.
- principalSwitch wall (1.87x, budget 2x): within, but 93% of budget —
  dispose 98.3 vs 4.7 (the in-action table/graph clear) plus build 352.8 vs
  230.0 (same per-known construction, one `replace`). No verdict moves by
  trimming dispose alone; not taken (Mc3 weighed the same trade and declined).
- rescope heap growth (1.274 vs ≤ 1.230, OVER by 0.044): retained grown
  state. Counted locally (1x boot → 2x `replace` → 1x `replace`: live cells
  59,313 → 129,239 → 130,150; resident issues 2,736 → 6,715 → 3,969;
  filings re-filed to 4,867): `residency.place` keeps "a row resident before
  stays", so ~1,200 rows the rule would keep cold stay resident with all
  their cells after the back-replace, and the back `replace` re-runs the
  fullSync filing over everything (back heap exceeds grown heap: 120.48 vs
  94.53 MB). Evicting resident-but-cold-by-rule rows on `replace` would move
  this but changes Ha3 eviction semantics across every gate that covers
  evict/re-add (the exact family that bit MobX, M4 §2): reported with the
  mechanism, not changed here. Candidate follow-up under POD-4545.

Per the coordinator's addendum this counts as a G6 gate result, not a
widened budget: coldBootstrap wall OVER at 2.47x, retained heap OVER at
3.43x, rescope heap growth OVER at 1.274 (budget 1.230); principalSwitch wall
within at 1.87x; switch heap growth within; rescope wall reported without
budget.
