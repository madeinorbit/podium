| Step | 1x index / rollup / row (ms) | 4x index / rollup / row (ms) |

are the next lever — the leased re-run will say with real numbers.

## 9. Clock verdict (the arm's named weak point)

F-clock is closed as INHERENT, bounded, CPU-only — stated plainly. The +60s
tick re-runs 3,589 settled bodies (pinned in `mobx.clock.test.tsx`) and
commits 0 rows / 0 renders: automatic tracking subscribes every row holding a
finished member to the coarse clock through its retention check and keeps it
subscribed, which is CORRECT (the +60d jump commits exactly the
still-visible band-movers — no stale clock rows by construction, against the
hand arm's 3 hand-maintained sensitivity sets). Unsubscribing means
hand-maintained sensitivity sets, i.e. becoming the hand arm and abandoning
the approach under comparison. Bound: ≤ rows holding finished members plus
defer carriers; consequence: pure-function bodies settling by equality. That
is the single largest behavioural difference the comparison found, and it is
a finding, not a fix.
