## 2. Attribution (counted from outside the pool)

Bootstrap observables (`arms/mobx/pool/bootstrap.test.ts`, MobX `spy` "add"
events during `create()` + `replace`, no models built), at the timed SHA:

- 1x lazy: 212,855 observables over 2,736 resident issues + 2,548 resident
  sessions (+ 2,131 cold issues + 1,756 cold sessions registered, never in
  tables). All-resident: 228,102. Lazy saves ~7%.
- By map: `IssueNode` 155,744 (73.2%), `SessionNode` 13,710 (6.4%), the rest
  tables/buckets/relations (<3% each). One `IssueNode` per KNOWN issue (4,867,
  hot or cold): `VisibleCollection.sync(knownIssueIds)` at every `replace`
  builds the node (32 computed annotations: 155,744 / 4,867 exactly) plus four firing reactions
  (visible, nested, formal, layout) before first paint.
- 4x lazy: 852,289 observables (linear in scale, 4.0x).

bootstrap, not tracking): the pool builds visibility nodes + reactions for
every known issue, including 2,131 cold ones no derivation will read until
first access.
