- 1x lazy: 212,855 observables over 2,736 resident issues + 2,548 resident

## 3. Fixes (STEP 2): no code change; the misses stay with their mechanism

Dispose-path review (the issue's "typical" leaks): `arm.dispose`
unsubscribes both channels, unmounts and clears all roots, then
`pool.dispose` stops every node reaction BEFORE clearing tables
(`VisibleCollection.clear`), clears groups, tables, graph, seats, selection
and read-states, drops models, residency and clock; the writable arm (Mc1/Mc2,
not on the measured page) unwires remotes/receipts too. The per-sample
survivor fence (WeakRefs on runtime, store, replica, cache, arm handle, row
source) passed all 20 switches: nothing of the old principal survives. No
listener, model or registry leak found to fix.

Considered and rejected: skipping the in-action table/graph clear on dispose
(stop reactions, drop references, let GC reclaim) would cut ~130 ms off the
switch wall (dispose 139.6 → ~10) but the wall would still read ~570 ms
against the 498.6 budget — no verdict moves, while dispose semantics shared
with every pool test would change. Not taken.

The three misses are construction, the cost Linear names for MobX, unchanged
since Mb4 (759.4 vs 775.7; 696.9 vs 639.8; 87.49 vs 81.07 — the small deltas
are the maintained grouping and write overlays landed since): one `IssueNode`
(32 computed annotations) plus four firing reactions (visible, nested,
formal, layout) per KNOWN issue at every `replace`, cold rows included —
4,867 nodes for 732 visible rows, evaluated before first paint (buildMs 640.6
vs control 109.0). Making nodes lazy (first access, like session nodes) needs
a visibility detector that does not walk the corpus — a redesign across every
fence/count lane other lanes own, not a lifecycle fix. Per the coordinator's
addendum this counts as a G6 gate result, not a widened budget: coldBootstrap
wall OVER at 3.56x (budget 1.1x), retained heap OVER at 3.88x (budget 1.1x),
principalSwitch wall OVER at 2.80x (budget 2x); switch/rescope heap growth
within; rescope wall reported without budget.
